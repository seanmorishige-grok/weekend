1Northwest Railway Museum Halloween Train (final weekend)

Snoqualmie / North Bend

Two-hour vintage train ride to the top of Snoqualmie Falls, with a 30-minute stop for cider, apple pressing, crafts and Eric Ode's music.

🚗 ~10 min to Snoqualmie Depot🕙 Trains every 2 hrs from 10:30$32 adults, $16 kids 2–12, under 2 free

Good for: Morning · ~2 hrs

Details: Last weekend, and Saturday is Halloween itself. Close to home and rain or shine, only slightly spooky, and every child gets a small gift. The 10:30 North Bend or 11:00 Snoqualmie train is back before nap.

☔ Rain plan · Runs rain or shine; both days look dry.
Good to know

What to bring. Costumes encouraged for all ages. Tickets are needed for everyone 2 and up.

Hours note. Arrive at least 30 minutes before departure. This is the last weekend; Santa trains start later in the season.

2Howl-O-Ween at Cougar Mountain Zoo

Issaquah

Halloween day at the small hillside zoo: trick-or-treating from enclosure to enclosure, a pumpkin scavenger hunt, a costume contest and Halloween treats for the animals.

🚗 ~25 min🕙 Sat 9:30–5Kids 2–12 free in costume

Good for: Morning · ~1.5–2 hrs

Details: Both kids get in free in costume, so it's mostly the adult tickets. The zoo is small and walkable, and the trick-or-treat route keeps a 2-year-old moving. A sunny Halloween morning is the best time to go.

☔ Rain plan · Mostly outdoors; Saturday looks sunny and dry.
Good to know

What to bring. Costumes (that's what makes the kids free) and a candy bag.

Hours note. Buying admission ahead is recommended. Children 15 and under must be with an adult.

4Remlinger Farms Fall Harvest Pumpkin Festival (closing weekend)

Carnation

Big farm fun park: 70+ rides and attractions, steam train, hay ride to Pumpkin Village, corn maze and the U-pick field.

🚗 ~25 min🕙 Sat & Sun 10–6$33.95 online / $34.95 gate

Good for: Either · ~2–3 hrs

Details: Closing weekend, with a costume contest on Halloween. Lots of rides for under-36" kids (Tiny Tots Train, Diggity Diggers, Mini Carousel, Bunny Hopper). The wristband allows re-entry, so you can go home for nap and come back after 3.

☔ Rain plan · Mostly outdoors; both days look dry, but the fields are still muddy.
Good to know

Parking & passes. Free on-site parking; arrive before 11 on Saturday for a short walk.

What to bring. Boots.

Hours note. Buy online: $1 less and Saturdays can sell out. Pony rides 11–4, check in by 3:30. Costume contest is Sat Oct 31.

7Happiest Halloween on Snoqualmie Ridge

Snoqualmie

Free Halloween-night trick-or-treating around the Ridge, with big costumed sets (Princess Castle, Pirate Ship), hot cider stands and indoor rooms at Church on the Ridge.

🚗 ~5 min🕙 Sat only 6–8Free

Good for: Afternoon · ~1 hr

Details: Our own neighborhood on Halloween night, built to be friendly rather than scary. The indoor Glo Party is for ages 2–5, so both kids fit. Do the castle and pirate ship, grab cider, and be home for bed.

☔ Rain plan · Indoor rooms at the church if it gets cold; Saturday evening looks dry.
Good to know

What to bring. Costumes, a treat bag and warm layers (it'll be in the 40s). A map of the stops is on the church's page.

Hours note. Starts at 6, a little later than our usual afternoon block.

10Snoqualmie Falls upper viewpoints

Snoqualmie

The 270-foot falls from the upper park's observation decks, a short stroll from the lot, with big spray after rain.

🚗 ~10 min🕙 Sunrise–sunsetFree

Good for: Either · 30–45 min

Details: The quickest big-wow outing from home: flat paved paths to the overlook, room for the 2-year-old to walk, and a fast exit if moods turn. Sunday afternoon looks sunny, a good easy outing after a big Halloween.

☔ Rain plan · Both days look dry.
Good to know

Parking & passes. Free lot across the street, or the paid lot next to the falls ($7 per hour). Keep valuables out of sight.
