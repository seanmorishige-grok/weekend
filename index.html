1Northwest Railway Museum Halloween Train

Snoqualmie / North Bend

Costumes-encouraged valley train with cider pressing, crafts, Eric Ode music, and a Train Shed stop — the hometown October classic.

🚗 ~8–12 min to Snoqualmie Depot🕙 Trains every 2 hrs from 10:30Adults $32

Good for: Sat or Sun morning · ~2 hrs

Details: Slightly spooky décor only, and the enclosed coaches run rain or shine. Ages 2 and 4 love the ride itself, plus cider pressing, apple coring, crafts and live music from Eric Ode at the Train Shed. Arrive about 30 minutes early.

☔ Rain plan · Coaches are enclosed; apple activities under Shed eaves; cider/crafts/music indoors — still go.
Good to know

What to bring. Costumes encouraged. No food or drink on the train.

Hours note. Often sells out, so buy ahead. Tickets are for a set date and time, no refunds. Everyone 2+ needs a ticket. Runs rain or shine. Boards at North Bend Depot (205 E McClellan St) or Snoqualmie Depot (38625 SE King St); check your ticket.

2Remlinger Farms Fall Harvest Pumpkin Festival

Carnation

The valley's big pumpkin festival: 70+ rides and attractions on one wristband, a steam train, hay ride to Pumpkin Village, corn maze and U-pick patch.

🚗 ~25 min🕙 Fri–Sun 10–6$33.95 ages 2–65 online

Good for: Sun morning · ~2–3 hrs

Details: Lots of the rides take under-36-inch riders with a grown-up, including the steam train, Tiny Tots Train and hay ride, so both kids get plenty to do. The first train leaves at 10:30. Sunday's sunny forecast makes it the better day.

☔ Rain plan · Saturday showers look light; if it's wet, go Sunday or swap to KidsQuest (#10).
Good to know

Parking & passes. Free on-site parking. On Saturdays, arrive before 11 for a short walk.

What to bring. Boots (it's working farmland), layers, and socks for the hay jump.

Hours note. Last admission 5 PM. Mid-October Saturdays sell out, so buy online. Pony rides ($7) are added to your tickets online; last pony check-in 3:30.

3Salmon season at the Issaquah Salmon Hatchery

Issaquah

Peak salmon run: watch Chinook and coho in Issaquah Creek and nose-to-nose through the fish ladder windows, then feed the trout.

🚗 ~20 min🕙 Dawn–duskFree

Good for: Sat or Sun afternoon · 45–90 min

Details: Chinook spawn through mid-October and coho arrive late September through November, so there should be fish in the creek. The viewing bridge, glassed-in fish ladder and aquarium room (eggs to baby salmon) are all short walks apart. Self-guided visits take 45–90 minutes.

☔ Rain plan · The aquarium room and 8-minute film room are indoors (8:30–4); the rest is outside.
Good to know

Parking & passes. Main lot: the second entrance heading west on Sunset Way. Overflow: the first entrance. Or use the garage next to the library across the street.

What to bring. A dollar or two to feed the trout (it's a $1 donation).

Hours note. Grounds are open dawn to dusk, but the indoor aquarium and film room close at 4.

9Olallie State Park — Weeks Falls

North Bend / Exit 38

Short forest walk to a 30-ft waterfall + riverside picnic — and Saturday is a statewide free Discover Pass day.

🚗 ~20–25 min🕙 Open 6:30–duskSat free day-use

Good for: Sat or Sun morning · ~1–1.5 hrs

Details: WTA lists the falls walk at about 1–2 miles round trip with modest gain from the South Fork picnic area; go at toddler pace and turn around whenever legs quit. Payoff: waterfall spray, river pebbles and picnic tables. Watch the cold river water with littles.

☔ Rain plan · Saturday's showers look light; Sunday is sunny but needs a Discover Pass. If it's pouring, swap to KidsQuest (#10) or LEGO Builders (#8).
Good to know

Parking & passes. Sat Oct 10 is a Discover Pass free day (World Mental Health Day). Sunday needs a Discover Pass ($10/day; there's a pay station).
