1Diwali at Bellevue Square

Bellevue

Free Festival of Lights celebration: Indian dance and music in Center Court, sand rangoli and sari displays, free henna, and a Diwali photo spot.

🚗 ~30 min🕙 Sat 11–6 (shows 12–5)Free

Good for: Afternoon · ~1–1.5 hrs

Details: Indoors, free and only on Saturday. Music and dancing hold a 2- and 4-year-old's attention in short bursts, and you can walk away anytime. Go after nap for the last couple of hours of performances, then an early dinner nearby.

☔ Rain plan · Indoors.
Good to know

Hours note. Live performances end at 5 PM; henna runs until 6.

3Coho at the Issaquah Salmon Hatchery + Apple Pie Picnic Story Stroll

Issaquah

Watch coho salmon in Issaquah Creek at the free hatchery, then follow a picture-book walk through historic downtown that starts at the library a block away.

🚗 ~20 min🕙 Dawn–dusk; stroll SatFree

Good for: Morning · ~1.5 hrs

Details: November is coho season, and Saturday morning is the driest window of the weekend. The hatchery viewing is short and close-up for little kids, and the story walk turns the stroll back into downtown into a book. Lunch on Front St before nap.

☔ Rain plan · Mostly outdoors. Saturday morning looks cloudy but mostly dry (19% rain).
Good to know

Hours note. The Story Stroll and book sale are Saturday only.

4Northwest Railway Museum train ride (Saturdays in November)

Snoqualmie / North Bend

The regular two-hour vintage train ride to the top of Snoqualmie Falls, with a 30-minute stop at the Train Shed Exhibit Hall.

🚗 ~10 min to Snoqualmie Depot🕙 Sat trains from 10:30Up to $28 per ticket

Good for: Morning · ~2 hrs

Details: The Halloween crowds are gone, so this is a calmer ride on the same route, five minutes from home. The 10:30 North Bend or 11:00 Snoqualmie train is back before nap; the 3:00 Snoqualmie train works after nap.

☔ Rain plan · Runs rain or shine.
Good to know

What to bring. No food, drinks or pets on the train.

Hours note. Buy tickets ahead; each train holds about 45 riders and sales are final. Santa trains start Nov 28.

5KidsQuest Children's Museum

Bellevue

Hands-on children's museum with an atrium climber, the Bellevue Mercantile pretend store, a building area, the Story Tree and an art studio.

🚗 ~30 min🕙 Sat 9:30–7, Sun 11:30–5$16.75 per person age 1+

Good for: Either · ~2 hrs

Details: The best rainy-day fallback, and both kids can play at their own level. Saturday's late close makes an after-nap visit easy. On Sunday it opens at 11:30, so pair it with an afternoon visit.

7Meadowbrook Farm Preserve

North Bend

Wide, flat trails across open meadows at the foot of Mount Si, where elk often graze.

🚗 ~10–15 min🕙 Dawn–duskFree

Good for: Morning · ~1 hr

Details: Close to home and easy for a 2-year-old. The paved, flat trail from the Interpretive Center toward Centennial Fields is 1.5 miles round trip, and you can turn around anytime. Elk are most often seen early or near dusk; watch from at least 100 feet.

☔ Rain plan · Open fields with little cover; best Saturday morning.
Good to know

Parking & passes. Free parking at the Interpretive Center, plus pullouts along SR 202 and Boalch Ave.

What to bring. Boots for the grassy Meadow Loop, which gets muddy in winter.

8Snoqualmie Falls upper viewpoints

Snoqualmie

The 270-foot falls from the upper park's observation decks, a short stroll from the lot, with big spray after rain.

🚗 ~10 min🕙 Sunrise–sunsetFree

Good for: Either · 30–45 min

Details: Sunday's rain should make the falls loud and full. It's a quick outing between showers: flat paved paths to the overlook and a fast exit if moods turn.

☔ Rain plan · Short and close to the car; bring rain jackets.
Good to know

9Olallie State Park: Weeks Falls

North Bend / Exit 38

Short forest walk to a 30-foot waterfall with riverside picnic tables.

🚗 ~20–25 min🕙 6:30–duskDiscover Pass

Good for: Morning · ~1–1.5 hrs

Details: WTA lists the falls walk at about 1–2 miles round trip with modest gain from the South Fork picnic area; go at toddler pace and turn around whenever legs quit. After this week's rain the falls should be running high. Keep littles back from the cold river.

☔ Rain plan · Forest trail, so light showers are fine with rain jackets; if it's pouring, swap to KidsQuest or the sports carnival.
Good to know

Parking & passes. Discover Pass required ($10/day or $45/year).
