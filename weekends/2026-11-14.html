1Snoqualmie Holiday Open House

Snoqualmie (Historic Downtown + Ridge)

Citywide holiday shopping day: shops and restaurants offer samples, specials and giveaways. Books and Bottles has a Letters to Santa writing station, and No Boat Brewing hosts a Holiday Maker's Market from noon to 5.

🚗 ~5–10 min🕙 Sat 11–6Free to walk around

Good for: Either · ~1.5 hrs

Details: A local event close to home. Specials kids will like include Chickadee Bakeshop holiday samples, a new peppermint milkshake at Snoqualmie Ice Cream and the Letters to Santa station at Books and Bottles. Do the Ridge before nap, or Historic Downtown and No Boat's market after nap with an early dinner from the Marigold Pizza truck.

☔ Rain plan · Mostly indoors in shops; short walks between them.
Good to know

Hours note. Saturday only. Specials vary by shop; the city's events page lists them.

2Tots & Trees planting at Pickering Barn

Issaquah

Drop-in tree and shrub planting for families with kids 6 and under, run by Trout Unlimited and Green Issaquah.

6Northwest Railway Museum train ride (Saturdays in November)

Snoqualmie / North Bend

Two-hour vintage train ride to the top of Snoqualmie Falls with a 30-minute stop at the Train Shed Exhibit Hall. Two Saturdays left before the Santa trains.

🚗 ~10 min to Snoqualmie Depot🕙 Sat trains from 10:30Up to $28 per ticket

Good for: Morning · ~2 hrs

Details: Close to home and runs rain or shine. The 10:30 North Bend or 11:00 Snoqualmie train is back before nap; the 3:00 Snoqualmie train works after nap.

☔ Rain plan · Runs rain or shine.
Good to know

What to bring. No food or drinks on board except capped water; no pets.

Hours note. Buy tickets ahead. Santa trains (North Pole Limited) start Nov 28.
