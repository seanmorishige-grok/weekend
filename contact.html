About & Contact

More Good Days is a weekly family weekend plans page. It is operated by Sean Morishige, sole proprietor, in Snoqualmie, Washington.

Text messages

One text is sent each Wednesday evening with a link to the family weekend plans page, More Good Days (seanmorishige-grok.github.io/weekend). Expect about 4–5 messages per month.

Recipients are family members who opted in. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

How to opt in

Family members opt in by asking Sean Morishige directly (verbal consent) or by texting the number that sends the weekly link. Details: text opt-in page.

Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help. Msg & data rates may apply.
