1Bigfoot Kids’ Book Festival

Redmond Senior & Community Center

Free one-day festival with 50+ kids’ authors, read-alouds, draw-alongs, and character photo ops.

🚗 ~30–40 min🕙 Sat only 10–4Free

Good for: Sat morning · ~1.5–2 hrs

Details: Listed for ages ~1–12. Unstructured drop-in energy: browse tables, catch a short read-aloud, snap a Gruffalo / Narwhal photo, leave when littles fade. Best unique Eastside find of the weekend that isn’t a farm.

☔ Rain plan · Fully indoor community center — this is already the rain plan.
Good to know

What to bring. Your own books to get signed (or pre-order books for signing).

Hours note. Sat Oct 10 only. Free, no tickets or registration.

2Olallie State Park — Weeks Falls (Discover Pass FREE Sat)

North Bend / Exit 38

Short forest walk to a 30-ft waterfall + riverside picnic — and Saturday is a statewide free Discover Pass day.

🚗 ~20–25 min🕙 Open 6:30–duskSat free day-use

Good for: Sat morning · ~1–1.5 hrs

Details: WTA lists ~1–2 mi RT with modest gain to the falls base from Homestead Valley / South Fork picnic area — keep it toddler-pace and turn around when legs quit. Concrete payoff: waterfall spray, river pebbles, picnic tables. Not Rattlesnake; no scramble required for the short falls walk. Watch cold river water with littles.

☔ Rain plan · Keep to a 20–30 min splash-and-look if drizzle; if pouring, swap to KidsQuest (#5) or LEGO Builders (#9).
Good to know

Parking & passes. Sat Oct 10 is a Discover Pass free day (World Mental Health Day). Sunday needs a Discover Pass ($10/day; there's a pay station).

Carnation

6Asahel Curtis Nature Trail

Snoqualmie Pass (Exit 47)

0.5-mile old-growth loop with a creek footbridge, ferns, and interpretive plant posts — big-tree payoff without a scramble.

🚗 ~25–30 min🕙 Day-useNorthwest Forest Pass or America the Beautiful required at trailhead

Good for: Sun morning · ~45–75 min

Details: WTA: 0.5 mi RT, ~180 ft gain, marked “good for kids.” Concrete payoff: old-growth canopy, burbling creek, numbered flora posts (grab the guide at the start). Turn around anytime. Not Rattlesnake; roots/mud possible after Sat rain — boots help. Skip if ice/snow shows up early at Pass elevation.

☔ Rain plan · Do only the bridge + first posts if dripping; otherwise KidsQuest or a downtown Snoqualmie indoor lunch + short Depot boardwalk.
Good to know

Parking & passes. Northwest Forest Pass required (Interagency passes also accepted). Sat's Discover Pass free day doesn't apply here.

7Northwest Railway Museum Halloween Train (early departure)

Snoqualmie / North Bend

Costumes-encouraged valley train with cider pressing, crafts, Eric Ode music, and a Train Shed stop — the hometown October classic.

🚗 ~8–12 min to Snoqualmie Depot🕙 Trains every 2 hrs from 10:30Adults $32

Good for: Sat morning · ~2 hrs

Details: Slightly spooky décor only; strollers OK (fold if possible); rain-or-shine coaches. Ages 2/4 love trains + cider + short crafts. Arrive 30 min early. No restrooms on the train — use depot bathrooms first.

☔ Rain plan · Coaches are enclosed; apple activities under Shed eaves; cider/crafts/music indoors — still go.
Good to know

What to bring. Costumes encouraged. No food or drink on the train.

Hours note. Often sells out, so buy ahead. Tickets are for a set date and time, no refunds. Everyone 2+ needs a ticket. Runs rain or shine. Boards at North Bend Depot (205 E McClellan St) or Snoqualmie Depot (38625 SE King St); check your ticket.

Nap conflicts

  • Halloween Train 1:00 PM Snoqualmie / corresponding NB afternoon departures — full 2-hr ride overlaps 1–3 PM nap; only worth it if you’re deliberately skipping nap for costumes + cider + Eric Ode.
  • Remlinger Farms Fall Harvest full day (Fri–Sun 10 AM–6 PM; GA ~$33.95 ages 2–65 online, under 2 free) — peak-color weekend with 70+ attractions; a morning wristband slice can still fit nap, but the “do everything” day does not.
  • Red Barn Farm last-entry 3 PM afternoon block — fine if nap is skipped or shifted; morning is cleaner.
  • Seattle Japanese Garden Family Saturday origami (11 AM–2 PM; youth 12 & under free 10 AM–2 PM) — cool unique find, but ~45–60 min drive each way makes the return collide with nap unless you skip it.
  • Small Frye at Frye Art Museum (10:15–11:15 AM, free, ages 2–5, registration required) — great age fit but Seattle drive + hard stop time is a stretch vs Eastside options.
  • Turkfest (Seattle Center, noon–5 PM both days) and SLU Fall Harvest Celebration (11 AM–2 PM) — neat free city festivals; drive + timing fight the nap window from Crest View.

Filler bank

Centennial Fields inclusive playground (Snoqualmie), Tollgate Farm Park (North Bend), Remlinger morning wristband, Snoqualmie Falls upper viewpoints, Muddy Boots Pumpkins in Duvall (maze: under 5 free; ages 6–15 $12; 16+ $17), JB Family Growers pumpkin/puzzle patch in Woodinville ($5 ages 2–11), Issaquah Fall Farm Fresh Market Sat 9 AM–2 PM at Historic Shell Station, Lowe’s Issaquah free kids workshop Sat 10 AM (firefighting plane build).
